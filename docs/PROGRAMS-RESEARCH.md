# Programs research and content boundary

## Product decision

The public `/activities` route remains a participatory explanation of how
English practice feels: speak, exchange, make, and stay. It is deliberately not
a calendar or programme database.

`Programs` is a separate publication surface:

- the public `/programs` route records what English Club runs, has completed,
  and is carefully preparing;
- `/admin/programs` manages programme records and their publication state;
- the old admin `Activity` screen is an owner audit trail, so its visible name
  becomes `Audit log` instead of pretending to manage club activities.

## Evidence gathered

### Documented records

1. UPT Library Universitas Jambi states that English Club was formally opened
   on 16 May 2024 in the UPT Library auditorium. The record describes group
   discussion, cultural performance, and language seminars, with participation
   open across proficiency levels.
   Source: <https://librarynew.unja.ac.id/english-club-upt-perpustakaan-resmi-di-bentuk/>
2. Universitas Jambi records a Sharing Session held through English Club on 20
   August 2025 on the fourth floor of the Hexagonal Building. It names three
   University of Leeds guests and reports 88 Universitas Jambi participants.
   Source: <https://www.unja.ac.id/upt-perpustakaan-unja-gelar-kegiatan-sharing-session-hadirkan-3-mahasiswa-university-of-leeds/>

### Mission-backed programme lines

The organisation's published About page lists training and workshops,
competition participation, an inclusive learning environment, community
service relevant to English, and collaboration with organisations or
institutions. These support programme directions, not invented dates,
frequencies, partners, outcomes, or completed-project claims.
Source: <https://englishclub.librarynew.unja.ac.id/about>

## Claims deliberately rejected

- Agriculture English Club activities are not attributed to this organisation.
- Photographs alone do not prove a partnership, event date, participant count,
  recurrence, or outcome.
- `Community English Service` is presented as an open direction until its
  partner, need, scope, safeguarding, date, and evidence are reviewed.
- Competition results and impact metrics are omitted until a primary record is
  available.

## Publication model

Every record has one delivery label:

- `completed`: a documented, dated record with an official source;
- `ongoing`: a programme line supported by the club's published mission;
- `planned`: an open direction, never rendered as completed work.

Convex stores immutable revisions. Editors prepare a working copy; publishers
choose the exact revision that becomes public. Archive is reversible and does
not erase the audit trail.

## Content and interface acceptance

- Activities and Programs never compete for the same job.
- The first public view explains the distinction in plain language.
- Completed records expose their source links next to the claim.
- The programme archive remains useful without photography.
- Admin copy says `working copy`, `published version`, and `no unpublished
  changes`; it avoids unexplained database terms such as `definition` or
  `active draft`.
- Mobile controls are at least 44px, the programme ledger has no horizontal
  overflow at 320px, and motion is limited to state/reveal support with a
  reduced-motion path.
