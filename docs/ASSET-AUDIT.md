# Asset Audit

Audit date: 25 August 2026
Scope: `assets/`
Coverage: 13 JPEG files and 1 MOV file

## Method and evidence boundary

The review inspected image pixels, file dimensions, EXIF, video streams, representative video frames, and crop safety. It does not infer names, roles, nationality, membership, learning outcomes, or formal partnerships from appearance. Camera dates conflict with visible event material in several files, so EXIF timestamps are not treated as event dates.

Temporary video frames were written to an untracked system temporary directory outside the repository. No project asset was edited during the audit.

## Visual groups

- `IMG_1903.JPG`, `IMG_2017.JPG`, `IMG_2028.JPG`, and `IMG_4945.JPG` show the event title "Leeds the Way: Bridging England and Indonesia."
- `IMG_3165.JPG` and `MVI_3166.MOV` show a donation handover at Rumah Qur'an Ghaza.
- Every `_MG_*` file shows an informal gathering in a room with library shelving. A visible poster reads "EC Incoming Day: Journey of the Golden Island."

## File-by-file review

### `IMG_1903.JPG`

**Visible content.** A five-person adult panel; one participant holds a microphone. A display shows "Leeds the Way: Bridging England and Indonesia" and a University of Leeds logo.

**Technical condition.** 6000 x 4000, approximately 3.02 MiB, ISO 100, shutter 1/6 second. Movement is soft and the indoor white balance is warm. A 16:9 crop is safe vertically, but the composition is too broad for a portrait crop. The frame does not offer a quiet area for text.

**Use.** Place inside an event article or beside text in a split section. Do not use as a full-bleed hero and do not overlay copy.

### `IMG_2017.JPG`

**Visible content.** A large posed group fills an auditorium. A table, bottle, and lounge chairs sit in the foreground.

**Technical condition.** 6000 x 4000, approximately 5.11 MiB, ISO 640, 16 mm. The file is reasonably clear; wide-angle distortion and edge crops need care. A 16:9 crop can remove ceiling and some foreground. A portrait crop would discard much of the group.

**Use.** About page, community-scale chapter, or event gallery. Keep text outside the photograph.

### `IMG_2028.JPG`

**Visible content.** A two-row posed group wearing lanyards and formal or batik clothing in the same auditorium.

**Technical condition.** 6000 x 4000, approximately 5.11 MiB, ISO 1250. The image is usable with moderate noise; the ceiling lights are bright. A wide crop is safe. A narrow mobile crop can retain only the central group.

**Use.** About or event-story support. Do not label people as committee members or organisers until roles are confirmed.

### `IMG_3165.JPG`

**Visible content.** Adults and children pose beneath a banner reading "Penyerahan Donasi", "Rumah Qur'an Ghaza", and "Voices of Joy, Seeds of Hope." The banner states Saturday, 2 November 2024.

**Technical condition.** 4608 x 3456, approximately 4.51 MiB, 4:3, ISO 800, shutter 1/13 second. Window highlights are clipped and the image is noisy and soft. A camera date printed into the frame reads `01/01/2014`, which conflicts with the banner. The banner and children both carry meaning, so aggressive cropping would remove evidence.

**Use.** Hold from the public release. It could support a social-impact article after consent from the event organiser and guardians. It is not suitable for the hero.

### `IMG_4945.JPG`

**Visible content.** A clearer, tighter view of the same panel as `IMG_1903.JPG`; the screen and five participants are readable.

**Technical condition.** 965 x 543, approximately 0.12 MiB, native 16:9. The composition is clean, but resolution is insufficient for a full-width display. Keep rendered width near 480 CSS pixels on a 2x screen.

**Use.** Article thumbnail or a small inline image.

### `MVI_3166.MOV`

**Visible content.** The donation group from `IMG_3165.JPG`, including children. For roughly 0-7.2 seconds the group poses and raises hands. Around 7.4 seconds the camera tilts upward; the end shows the ceiling.

**Technical condition.** 1280 x 720, 25 fps, H.264 at roughly 12.8 Mbps, PCM stereo 48 kHz, 9.36 seconds, approximately 17.36 MiB. The embedded 2014 creation time conflicts with the visible banner. The bitrate and PCM audio make the file unnecessarily large for the web.

**Use.** Hold from the public release. If consent later covers faces and audio, trim to 0-7.2 seconds, transcode to MP4 H.264/AAC or WebM, create a poster image, set `preload="metadata"`, expose controls, and add captions where speech is meaningful. Never autoplay audible media.

## Metadata and privacy

### Remove before publication

- `IMG_4945.JPG` contains precise GPS coordinates, altitude, GPS time, and device details. Those values are intentionally omitted from this repository; the public derivative must strip the complete metadata block.
- `IMG_1903.JPG`, `IMG_2017.JPG`, and `IMG_2028.JPG` contain an artist field plus camera-body and lens serial data. Exact identifiers are intentionally omitted.
- Every `_MG_*` file contains a camera-body serial identifier. Exact identifiers are intentionally omitted.
- Other Canon files expose a GPS version block even when no coordinates were readable. Strip all EXIF instead of trying to retain selected fields.

### Human and organisational privacy

- Lanyards or IDs may be legible in the 6000 px masters of `IMG_2017.JPG` and `IMG_2028.JPG`.
- Screens in `IMG_1903.JPG` and `IMG_4945.JPG` include speaker images or names and third-party marks.
- Posters and QR codes in `_MG_*` may point to old or third-party information.
- Every asset contains recognisable faces. `IMG_3165.JPG` and `MVI_3166.MOV` clearly contain children and require guardian or organiser consent.
- Video audio may identify people even if a visual crop is approved.
- A third-party logo visible in a photograph does not prove a formal partnership or endorsement.

## Derivative policy

- Keep masters outside `public/`.
- Strip all EXIF, XMP, GPS, and device identifiers.
- Produce responsive AVIF and WebP derivatives at 640, 960, 1440, and 1920 px where the source quality supports them.
- Record a desktop and mobile focal point for each published image.
- Store factual alt text, `rightsStatus`, `consentStatus`, and `captureDateVerified` alongside the media record.
- Prefer local static imports for the first-release archive because Next.js can infer dimensions and blur placeholders.
- Do not publish any file until consent status is explicit.
