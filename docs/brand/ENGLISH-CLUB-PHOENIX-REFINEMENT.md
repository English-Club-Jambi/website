# English Club Phoenix Refinement

Status: executed identity direction, ready for stakeholder review and final trademark clearance.

## Refined creative brief

Redesign the existing English Club phoenix as a contemporary identity system. Preserve the upward head, circular sweep, and S-shaped body that make the former mark recognizable, but remove the enclosed ring, repeated feather cuts, ornamental tail, and serif lockup. The new mark must express freedom through an open silhouette and fire through one rising, asymmetric flame. It must remain clear at 20 pixels, translate to one colour, and avoid the visual language of esports mascots, heraldic crests, or stock phoenix emblems.

## Chosen idea: Open Flame Phoenix

The mark uses two dominant fields and one continuous body gesture:

- the left wing opens outward rather than completing a circle;
- the central body rises in one continuous gesture toward the head;
- the taller right flame breaks the old boundary and carries the energy upward.

The opening is intentional. It turns freedom into structure instead of adding a separate symbol. The flame is part of the bird rather than a decorative effect. The body begins low and rises through the centre: resurrection is expressed as direction and transformation, not as another ornament.

Two restrained negative-space cuts provide the final amount of complexity. The wing crescent recalls the layered feathers of the former mark; the flame cut gives renewal a second beat. They do not alter the approved outer silhouette.

## Philosophy in the form

| Principle | Structural expression |
| --- | --- |
| Freedom | The wings do not close into a ring or badge. The silhouette remains open to the top and sides. |
| Fire | The orange field is a taller, independent flame rather than a colour applied to a feather. |
| Resurrection | The central phoenix rises from the lowest point and becomes the head; the form reads from ember to ascent. |
| Voice | The head turns outward and the open interval between bird and flame gives the mark room to speak. |

No pattern beyond these two cuts belongs inside the master logo. Ash, sparks, sun discs, extra feathers, or rebirth rings would weaken the 20-pixel proof and make the philosophy literal. Resurrection can be expanded through motion or a secondary campaign pattern, but those applications must never alter the master geometry.

## What changed from the former logo

| Former characteristic | Refined response |
| --- | --- |
| Layered feather cuts | One broad wing plane with a single heritage crescent |
| Closed circular enclosure | Open, asymmetric silhouette |
| Long ornamental tail | Stable, compact lower contour |
| Detailed bird illustration | One stable outer silhouette with two controlled cuts |
| Serif all-caps wordmark | Sturdy sentence-case grotesk |
| Dark teal character | Original Teal retained as a first-class colourway |

## Colour systems

- Indigo: `#4937E8`
- Flame orange: `#F05A00`
- Ink: `#111827`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

This is the closest fit with the current website and gives the clearest separation between freedom and fire.

### Original Teal

- Deep teal: `#073F52`
- Sea teal: `#0C708D`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

This palette directly retains the strongest colour memory from the former logo without reproducing its gradient. It is a first-class official colourway for continuity-led applications, alumni material, archives, and institutional contexts.

## Usage rules

- Use Relay as the default website mark.
- Use the monochrome artwork below 20 pixels or where only one ink is available.
- Never redraw the flame, close the free-standing wings into a circle, add more feather cuts, or add an eye.
- Do not add glow, bevels, outlines, or drop shadows. Preserve the supplied artwork rather than recreating its tonal treatment.
- Do not add ash, sparks, a sun disc, or a resurrection ring to the mark.
- Keep clear space equal to at least one quarter of the symbol width.
- Do not combine the UNJA logo inside this mark. Institutional attribution remains a separate lockup relationship.

## Rounded secondary mark

The Phoenix Roundel places the unchanged master pattern inside one circular field. It is intended for social avatars, stamps, merchandise, and other naturally circular placements. The circle is an application container, not a replacement master logo, and it must never be turned into a feather ring, university seal, or text badge.

## Motion principle

When motion is appropriate, resurrection can be shown in three restrained beats: a small ember appears, the central body rises, and the two outer fields open. The final frame must be the unchanged static logo. Avoid particle bursts, looping flames, bounce, or continuous wing flapping, and provide an immediate reduced-motion state.

## Evaluation

The final pattern rejects generic crest symmetry while recovering a small amount of the former logo's layered character. The same outer geometry and two cuts are present in Relay, Original Teal, monochrome, and roundel applications. The free-standing silhouette remains identifiable without colour, and the open wing, rising flame, and low-to-high body path carry freedom, fire, and resurrection without added symbols.
